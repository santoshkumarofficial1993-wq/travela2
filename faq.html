Questions, answered without the soft edges

If something you need is not here, call us. We would rather spend ten minutes on the phone than have you guess.

Booking and payment

How do I book a group departure?

Send an enquiry or call. We will confirm the seat is genuinely available, send a written summary of what you are buying, and take a $600 deposit per person. Nothing is charged before you have that summary in writing.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover and ACH bank transfer. Card details are handled by a PCI-compliant payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.

When is the balance due?

60 days before departure. Bookings made inside 60 days are payable in full at the time of booking. Interest-free instalment plans are available on request and cost nothing extra.

Do you charge a planning fee?

Only for custom trips: a flat $290, once per trip, regardless of party size or length. It is credited in full against your balance if you book. Group departures have no planning fee.

Are prices per person or per party?

Per person, in U.S. dollars, based on two people sharing a room. Single-occupancy amounts are printed on every trip page rather than quoted later.

Changes and cancellations

Can I cancel and get my money back?

In full, for 14 days after you pay the deposit, for any reason. After that a sliding schedule applies. The complete table with dates and amounts is on the refund and cancellation policy page and is repeated in your booking confirmation.

What if Firstlight cancels the trip?

You choose between a full refund of everything paid to us, or a transfer to another departure with any price difference waived. We will also reimburse documented change fees on flights booked through us. We cannot reimburse third-party flights, which is one reason we recommend insurance.

Can I change my dates after booking?

Free within the first 14 days. After that, date changes are treated as a cancellation and rebooking under the published schedule, though we will always try to move a deposit rather than keep it if the supplier lets us.

On the trip

How many people will be in my group?

Twelve maximum, ten on Japan and Peru. Most departures run with eight or nine. We do not add a thirteenth traveler under any circumstances, including for a full-price late booking.

What does the activity rating mean?

Easy is under two miles of walking a day on mostly level ground. Moderate is two to five miles daily on uneven surfaces. Active adds sustained climbs, altitude or full days on foot. Every trip page states the hardest single day so you can judge for yourself.

Can you accommodate mobility needs, allergies or dietary requirements?

Frequently, yes, and we will tell you honestly when we cannot. Some of our lodging is in old buildings without elevators and some routes have no vehicle access at all. Tell us early and we will either adapt the trip or point you at one that suits better.

Is tipping expected?

No. Guides and drivers are paid a full day rate by us. Tips are never solicited and never assumed, and no guide's income depends on them.

Are there optional extras sold during the trip?

No upsells and no commission stops. If an optional activity exists on a free afternoon, your guide will tell you the price and how to book it directly. We take nothing from it.

Solo travelers, families and accessibility

Is it awkward to join alone?

About a third of our travelers book solo, and there is no forced room sharing. Single rooms are offered at our cost. Group dinners are optional every night, not a schedule you are trapped in.

Do you take children?

Minimum age is 14 on most departures and 16 on the Peru and Norway routes, because of altitude and walking distance. Private family trips have no minimum age and we plan them around whoever is coming.

What is your accessibility position?

We describe every trip honestly rather than claiming universal access. Some of our routes are genuinely unsuitable for wheelchair users; some can be adapted. Our accessibility statement covers both the trips and this website.

Documents, insurance and safety

Do I need travel insurance?

It is not a condition of booking, but we recommend it strongly and most travelers buy it. We can introduce you to a licensed underwriter. We are not insurance brokers, we do not advise on policy terms, and we receive no commission on any policy you buy.

Who handles visas and entry requirements?

You do, and we help. Requirements depend on your passport and change frequently. We send the current rules for your nationality with your booking confirmation and remind you again at 90 days. Final responsibility for entry documents rests with the traveler.

What happens in an emergency?

A staffed 24-hour line, not an answering service. Every guide carries a satellite communicator on remote sections and holds a current wilderness first aid certification. Serious medical evacuation is covered by your insurance, not by us, which is the main reason we push you to buy it.

How do you handle my personal data?

We collect what a booking requires and no more, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with advertisers. California residents have specific rights to access and delete. Details are in the privacy policy.

Still not answered?

Call and ask. There is no phone tree and no script, and you will speak to someone who plans trips rather than someone who sells them.
