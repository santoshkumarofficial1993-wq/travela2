Disclaimer and advertising disclosure

Who pays us, what that does and does not influence, and how far you should trust anything published here.

Effective date: 1 January 2026.

1. General information only

Everything published on firstlighttravel.co is general information about travel. It is not personal advice, and it is not medical, legal, financial, immigration or insurance advice. Conditions in every destination change, sometimes overnight. Verify anything that matters against an official source before you rely on it: your government's travel advisory, the destination's immigration authority, your doctor, and your insurer.

We write in good faith and correct errors when we find them. We make no warranty that any page is complete, current or free of mistakes, and we accept no liability for a decision made solely on the basis of something you read here.

2. Editorial independence

Our guide articles are written by Firstlight planners and guides. No advertiser, affiliate partner or travel supplier reviews an article before publication, pays for placement in one, or has any right to request a change afterwards. If we recommend a hotel, a route or a month, it is because we would tell a friend the same thing.

We are not a neutral party about our own trips. Where an article touches a destination we sell, we say so in the article itself, in the first section, rather than burying it here.

3. Affiliate link disclosure

Some articles contain affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may receive a commission from the merchant at no additional cost to you. The price you pay is identical to the price you would pay going direct.

  • Affiliate links are labelled in the article where they appear. We do not hide them behind a single line at the bottom of the page.
  • We link only to products or services we have used or would use ourselves.
  • Commission rate plays no part in whether or where something is recommended, and we do not accept payment for a specific recommendation.
  • We do not accept free products in exchange for coverage.

This disclosure is made in line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255).

4. Advertising disclosure

Guide pages on this site display third-party advertising. Ad units are visually separated from editorial content and are labelled "Advertisement". We do not create ad units that resemble our own navigation, buttons or article links.

  • Advertisers do not see, receive or influence your trip enquiry.
  • An advertisement appearing on this site is not an endorsement by Firstlight of the advertiser, the product, or any claim made in the ad.
  • Ads are served programmatically by third parties, including Google, based on their own systems. We do not review each individual ad before it appears. If you see an ad that is misleading or offensive, tell us at hello@firstlighttravel.co and we will report it to the network.
  • How advertising cookies work, and how to switch them off, is covered in the cookie policy.

5. Reviews and testimonials

Traveler quotes on this site come from post-trip surveys and are published with written permission. They are edited only for length, never to change meaning, and we do not pay for them or offer discounts in exchange. They describe one person's experience of one departure. Your trip will differ, because weather, group and timing differ.

6. Prices and availability

Prices shown are per person in U.S. dollars, based on two people sharing, and exclude international airfare unless a page says otherwise. They are current at the time of publication and are not a binding offer. The only price that binds either of us is the one in your written booking confirmation. Availability shown on trip pages is updated manually and can lag by a day or two, which is why we confirm a seat before taking a deposit.

7. Photography

Photographs on this site are a mix of images taken by our guides and licensed stock photography. Landscape and destination images are illustrative of the region and are not necessarily photographs of the specific property, viewpoint, or conditions you will encounter on a given date. Where a photograph shows a named property on a trip page, we say so in the caption.

8. External links

We link to third-party websites where they are genuinely useful, including road and weather services in destination countries. We do not control those sites and we are not responsible for their content, accuracy or privacy practices. A link is not an endorsement of everything the linked site publishes.

9. Travel involves risk

Our trips include walking on uneven ground, exposure to weather, altitude on some routes, and travel by road, rail and boat in countries with different safety standards from the United States. We manage those risks carefully and we do not eliminate them. Assess your own fitness and health honestly, tell us anything relevant at booking, and carry insurance that covers medical treatment and evacuation.

10. Questions or corrections

If something on this site is wrong, tell us and we will fix it. Email hello@firstlighttravel.co or write to Firstlight Travel Co. LLC, 1420 5th Avenue, Suite 2200, Seattle, WA 98101, United States.
