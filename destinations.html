Eight regions, and the honest reason for each

We add a country when someone on the team has lived and worked there long enough to argue about restaurants. That is the whole criterion, and it is why the list is short.

8 regions shown

Iceland

Ring Road and the eastern fjords

Best March to April and late September to October, when the buses have gone and the roads are still open. From $4,180 for nine days.

See the Iceland trip

Japan

Kyoto, Kanazawa, Nakasendo

April or November. From $6,240.

Portugal

Lisbon to the Alentejo coast

May or September. From $3,480.

Peru

Sacred Valley, slowly

June to August, dry and cold at night. From $5,120.

Norway

Fjords by ferry and foot

June to August only. From $4,760.

Morocco

Fes, the Atlas, the coast

March or October. From $3,940.

Vietnam

Hanoi to Hoi An by rail

January to March. From $4,320.

United States

Utah and northern Arizona

April or October. From $2,760.

No regions match that season

We only run a region when the weather is genuinely worth the airfare. Try another season, or tell us your dates and we will say honestly whether it is a good idea.
