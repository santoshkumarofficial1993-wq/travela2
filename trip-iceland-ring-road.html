Iceland Ring Road, Shoulder Season

Nine days counter-clockwise from Reykjavik, timed for the two windows when the roads are open and the coaches are gone.

Why we run this in March and October

In July the Ring Road carries more traffic than it was built for and every guesthouse between Vik and Hofn is full six months ahead. In March and October the same route is quiet, the light is long and low for most of the day, and the small places that close for high season are open for locals. The trade is weather. Some days are bright and still. Some are horizontal rain. We build a spare afternoon into the route for exactly that reason.

A promise we will not makeNobody can guarantee the northern lights. Aurora is possible on this itinerary and we will wake the group for it, but it is weather and solar activity, not a scheduled event. Any operator who guarantees it is selling you something they cannot deliver.

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrive Reykjavik

    Airport pickup at any hour, a walk through the old harbour if you have slept, and dinner together at 7pm so nobody eats their first meal alone in a hotel room.

    Dinner includedReykjavik, 2 nights
  2. Day 2

    Reykjanes and the geothermal coast

    A short drive south to the lava fields and steam vents, then back to the city with the afternoon free. Roughly four miles of easy walking on marked paths.

    Breakfast, lunch4 miles walking
  3. Day 3

    South coast to Vik

    Two waterfalls before the day trippers arrive, a glacier tongue, and the black beach at Reynisfjara with a guide who will explain exactly why people drown there and where not to stand.

    Breakfast, dinnerVik, 1 night
  4. Day 4

    Skaftafell and the glacier lagoon

    A morning walk into the national park, then east to the lagoon in the late afternoon when the tour fleet has turned around. Icebergs on the beach opposite at dusk.

    BreakfastHofn, 1 night
  5. Day 5

    The eastern fjords

    The stretch most itineraries fly over. Fishing villages of two hundred people, a road that hugs every inlet, and a guesthouse where dinner is whatever came off the boat.

    Breakfast, dinnerSeydisfjordur, 2 nights
  6. Day 6

    The long walk, or the long morning

    A seven-mile ridge walk above the fjord for those who want it, with a guide. Everyone else gets a village, a bakery, a swimming pool heated by the ground, and no obligation.

    BreakfastOptional 7 miles
  7. Day 7

    North to Myvatn

    Across the highland edge to the geothermal lake district. Afternoon in the nature baths, which are the version of the Blue Lagoon that locals actually use.

    Breakfast, lunchMyvatn, 1 night
  8. Day 8

    Akureyri and the whale coast

    North coast harbours, a botanical garden that has no business existing at this latitude, and the spare afternoon we hold back for whatever the week has thrown at us.

    Breakfast, dinnerAkureyri, 1 night
  9. Day 9

    Fly to Reykjavik, depart

    A 45-minute domestic hop back to the capital, included in your price, with airport transfer timed to your outbound flight.

    BreakfastDomestic flight included

How fit do I need to be?

Moderate means two to five miles of walking on most days, on uneven ground, sometimes in wind and rain, with the option to sit any of it out. There is one seven-mile day and it is genuinely optional. If you can walk a hilly three miles at home without dreading it, this trip works. If you are not sure, call us and describe a walk you did recently. We would rather talk you out of the wrong trip than take your deposit.

Questions specific to this trip

Will I see the northern lights?

Possibly. Both departure windows fall inside aurora season and five of the eight nights are outside city light. Your guide checks the forecast nightly and will wake the group if it is worth it. It is not guaranteed and we will not pretend otherwise.

What is the weather actually like in March and October?

Expect 30°F to 45°F, wind, and a mix of clear days and rain. October has around ten hours of daylight, March around twelve and rising fast. We send a tested packing list six weeks out. You will need waterproof layers, not warmth alone.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Yes, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten free and most allergies. Tell us at booking so we can brief the guesthouses. Rural Iceland has limited options on short notice, which is exactly why we ask early.

Is there a single supplement?

A private room for all eight nights is $840. There is no forced room sharing and no penalty for travelling alone. If you would like a roommate we can ask the group, but we never assign one.
