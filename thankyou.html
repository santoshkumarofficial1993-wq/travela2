How a Firstlight trip gets built

Three conversations, then a document you can actually read. We charge a flat $290 planning fee for custom work and credit it back in full if you book a trip with us.

Talk

A 30-minute call about how you like to move: early starts or slow mornings, restaurants or markets, hotels or family-run guesthouses. No sales script.

Draft

You get a day-by-day itinerary with named properties, real walking distances, and the full cost broken into lines you can question. Two revisions are included.

Go

Deposit holds the dates. Your planner stays reachable by phone in your own time zone for the whole trip, including the hours nobody wants to need help.

Questions we get before booking

Do you charge a fee to plan a custom trip?

Yes. Custom planning is a flat $290, charged once, per trip, regardless of length or party size. If you book the trip through us, the full $290 is credited against your balance. Group departures listed on the trips page carry no planning fee.

Are flights included in the price?

International airfare is not included. Every trip page states exactly what is included and what is not. We will book your flights on request at cost with no service charge, or you can book them yourself and send us the itinerary.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Deposits are fully refundable for 14 days. After that, a sliding schedule applies based on how close you are to departure. The complete schedule with dates and amounts is on the refund and cancellation policy page.

Is this suitable if I travel alone?

About a third of our travelers book solo. There is no forced room sharing. Single-occupancy rooms are offered at our cost and the amount is printed on each trip page before you book.
