Privacy policy

This policy explains what Firstlight Travel Co. LLC collects about you, why, who else sees it, and what you can require us to do about it.

Effective date: 1 January 2026. Applies to www.firstlighttravel.co and to bookings made by phone or email.

The short versionWe collect what a trip enquiry and a booking require. We do not sell personal information. Advertising and analytics cookies only run if you agree to them, and you can change that decision at any time from the footer of any page.

1. Who we are

Firstlight Travel Co. LLC ("Firstlight", "we", "us") is a travel company registered in Washington State, United States, with a registered office at 1420 5th Avenue, Suite 2200, Seattle, WA 98101. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

For any privacy question or request, write to privacy@firstlighttravel.co or to the postal address above marked for the attention of the Privacy Contact.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry details: name, email address, phone number, destination, dates, party size and whatever you write in the message field.
  • Booking details: postal address, date of birth, passport details where a supplier requires them, emergency contact, and traveler names.
  • Health and dietary information you choose to share so we can plan safely. This is sensitive information and we treat it accordingly, sharing only the specific detail a given supplier needs.
  • Payment information: processed by a PCI-DSS compliant payment provider. We receive a confirmation and the last four digits of the card. We never receive or store the full card number or the security code.
  • Correspondence: emails, letters and notes from phone calls relating to your trip.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and time on page.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, as described in section 6 and in the cookie policy.

Information from other sources

Occasionally we receive information from a travel companion booking on your behalf, or from a supplier confirming an arrangement. We assume anyone booking for you has your permission to share your details.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers for their own purposes. We do share it with:

  • Travel suppliers who need it to deliver your trip: hotels and guesthouses, ground transport operators, guides, airlines and rail operators. Only the details each one needs.
  • Payment processors for card and ACH transactions.
  • Technology providers who host this website, send our email, and store our booking records under contract, acting on our instructions.
  • Advertising and analytics providers, including Google, as described in section 6.
  • Insurers and emergency assistance providers where you or your policy involves them.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where the law requires it.

Delivering an international trip means transferring information outside the United States, to the country you are travelling to. Where the GDPR applies, those transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

5. Cookies

We use a small number of necessary cookies to make the site work, and, with your permission, analytics and advertising cookies. You choose when the consent banner first appears, and you can revisit that choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. Necessary cookies cannot be switched off because the site does not function without them. The full list, with names and lifespans, is in the cookie policy.

6. Third-party advertising, including Google

Guide pages on this site carry display advertising. The following applies to that advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info and optout.networkadvertising.org.
  • Where consent is required, advertising cookies are only set after you accept them in our consent banner. If you choose "Necessary only", we signal that to Google using Consent Mode and no advertising or analytics storage is used.
  • We do not pass your name, email address, phone number, passport details, or any enquiry content to advertising vendors. Advertisers never see your booking.

Third-party advertising vendors have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Google's is published at policies.google.com/privacy.

8. Your rights

All visitors

Ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email privacy@firstlighttravel.co. We reply within 30 days and we do not charge a fee. We may need to verify your identity before acting, which usually means confirming details of an existing booking.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, you have the right to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the right to delete it, the right to correct inaccuracies, the right to opt out of sale or sharing, and the right to limit use of sensitive personal information. You have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these.

Firstlight does not sell personal information and has not sold personal information in the preceding 12 months. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. Use of advertising cookies may constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA; declining them in our banner, or choosing "Necessary only", is the opt-out mechanism for this site. We also honour the Global Privacy Control signal where your browser sends one.

You may designate an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf, with written permission we can verify.

Other U.S. states

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have broadly equivalent rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and opt-out, including a right to appeal a refused request. Contact us at the address above and state which law you are relying on.

UK and European Economic Area

If you are in the UK or EEA you additionally have the rights to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

9. Security

Booking data is held in access-controlled systems with encryption in transit and at rest. Access is limited to staff who need it for a specific trip. Payment card data never touches our systems. We test our own systems periodically and we require contractual security commitments from our processors. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the relevant regulator within the timeframes the law requires.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. Travelers under 18 are booked by a parent or guardian who provides their details. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other sites

Our guide articles link to external websites, some of which are affiliate links that are labelled where they appear. Once you follow a link, the destination site's privacy policy governs. We are not responsible for the content or practices of sites we do not operate.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this page when our practices change. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects how we use information you have already given us, we will email people with active bookings rather than rely on you re-reading this page.
