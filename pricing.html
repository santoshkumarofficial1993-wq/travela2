What it costs, and where the money goes

Travel pricing is usually vague on purpose. This page is the opposite. Read it before you talk to us and you will know almost everything a sales call would tell you.

Two ways to travel with us

Group departures are already priced and dated. Custom trips are built from scratch for you, your family or your friends, and carry a flat planning fee that comes back to you if you book.

We do not take supplier commissionHotels, guides and transport are billed to you at the rate we pay, plus a stated operating margin. That margin is the same whether you stay in a guesthouse or a design hotel, so nobody here has a reason to push you upward.

Our business model

Every dollar Firstlight earns comes from one of four places

If you are wondering whether advice on this site is influenced by who pays us, this is the honest answer.

Trip operating margin

The largest share. A stated percentage on top of what we pay suppliers, disclosed on request for any custom quote. It is flat, so upgrading your hotel does not increase what we make.

Custom planning fees

The $290 flat fee. It pays for planner hours on trips that never get booked, which is roughly one in three. Credited back in full when you do book.

Advertising on this site

Guide pages carry display advertising, served by third parties including Google. Ad slots are labelled. Advertisers get no say in what we write and never see your enquiry.

Affiliate links, stated plainly

A small number of guide articles link to gear or booking sites that pay us a commission if you buy. Those links are labelled in the article where they appear. They never change the price you pay, and we do not link to anything we would not recommend without the commission. Full detail sits in the disclaimer.

Paying, and changing your mind

Accepted methods

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and ACH bank transfer. Card payments are processed by a PCI-compliant provider. We never store your card number.

Payment schedule

Deposit at booking, balance 60 days before departure. Bookings made inside 60 days are payable in full. Instalment plans available on request at no extra cost.

Currency and fees

All prices and charges are in U.S. dollars. We add no card surcharge. Your bank may add a foreign transaction fee on ACH transfers from outside the United States.

If you cancel

Full refund within 14 days of deposit. After that a published sliding schedule applies, with the exact dates and amounts written into your booking confirmation.
