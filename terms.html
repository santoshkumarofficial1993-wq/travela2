5. Booking and payment

  • A booking is confirmed when we issue a written confirmation and receive the deposit. Group departures carry a $600 per-person deposit; custom trips carry 25% of the trip cost.
  • The balance is due 60 days before departure. Bookings made inside 60 days are payable in full at the time of booking.
  • If the balance is not received by the due date and we cannot reach you after two written attempts, we may treat the booking as cancelled by you under the refund policy.
  • Custom trip planning carries a flat $290 fee, credited in full against your balance if you go on to book.
  • All prices are in U.S. dollars. Prices are per person, based on two people sharing, and exclude international airfare unless the trip page says otherwise.
  • Once confirmed in writing, your trip price will not increase, with one exception: a government-imposed tax, levy or fuel surcharge introduced after confirmation may be passed through at cost, with evidence, and you may cancel without penalty if the increase exceeds 8% of the trip price.

6. Changes and cancellations

Your rights and the amounts involved are set out in full on the refunds and cancellations page, which forms part of these terms. In short: you may cancel for a full refund within 14 days of paying the deposit, and a published sliding schedule applies after that.

We may change an itinerary where safety, weather, road closures, supplier failure or local conditions require it. We will always try to deliver an equivalent or better experience and we will tell you as soon as we know. If we make a significant change before departure, meaning a change of destination country, a reduction in trip length, or a downgrade in the standard of most lodging, you may accept it, transfer to another departure, or cancel for a full refund.

We may cancel a departure that does not reach its minimum of four travelers. Where that happens we will tell you at least 45 days before departure and you may take a full refund or transfer with any price difference waived.

7. Passports, visas and health

You are responsible for holding a valid passport, any required visa, and any vaccination or health documentation for every country on your itinerary, including transit countries. We provide current guidance for U.S. passport holders with your confirmation and again at 90 days, but requirements change and the final responsibility is yours. We cannot refund a trip you cannot join because of a document problem.

8. Insurance

Travel insurance is not a condition of booking and we strongly recommend it. We are not insurance brokers, we do not advise on cover, and we receive no commission on any policy. Medical evacuation, trip interruption, and loss of personal belongings are your insurer's responsibility, not ours.

9. Conduct on a trip

Small groups only work when everyone can live with everyone. We ask travelers to be on time, to follow safety instructions from guides, and to treat local people, staff and each other decently. Harassment, discrimination, threats, illegal activity, or behaviour that endangers the group are grounds for removal from a trip without refund and at your own cost for onward travel. This is rare and we do not apply it lightly.

You are responsible for judging whether that suits your health and fitness, and for telling us at booking about any medical condition, mobility limitation, allergy or dietary requirement that affects your participation.
