The same inclusion list on every trip

If something is not on the left column, assume you are paying for it separately. We would rather set that expectation now than at a hotel desk.

In the price

  • All lodging

    Named on the itinerary before you pay a deposit.

  • Ground transport

    Trains, ferries, private vans and all airport transfers on trip days.

  • Resident guides

    Paid a day rate by us. They earn nothing from where you shop or eat.

  • Listed meals and entries

    Every included meal and ticket is marked day by day.

Not in the price

  • International airfare

    We will book it at cost with no service charge if you want us to.

  • Travel insurance

    Strongly recommended. Offered through a licensed third-party underwriter.

  • Visas and entry fees

    Requirements differ by passport. We send the current rules with your confirmation.

  • Unlisted meals and tips

    Guide gratuities are never expected and never solicited.
