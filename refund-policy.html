Refunds and cancellations

Written before you book rather than quoted after you ask. This page forms part of the terms of service and is repeated verbatim in every booking confirmation.

Effective date: 1 January 2026.

Fourteen days, no reason neededCancel within 14 days of paying your deposit and you get everything back, including the custom planning fee. No questions, no restocking charge, no travel credit instead of money.

1. If you cancel

Cancellations must be made in writing to hello@firstlighttravel.co. The effective date is the date we receive your email, not the date it was sent. We acknowledge every cancellation within one business day; if you have not heard from us, assume we have not received it and call.

Why the schedule tightens

Not because it is standard practice. As departure approaches we have already paid deposits to guesthouses, blocked guide days that cannot be resold, and in several regions committed to non-refundable rail and ferry allocations. The percentages above approximate what we can no longer recover. Where we recover more than expected, we refund the difference; that happens a few times a year and we do it without being asked.

2. If we cancel

You choose, and the choice is yours alone:

  • A full refund of everything you have paid us, processed within 10 business days, or
  • A transfer to any other Firstlight departure within 24 months, with any price difference waived in your favour.

We will also reimburse documented airline change or cancellation fees on flights we booked for you. We cannot reimburse flights or hotels you booked independently, which is the single strongest argument for buying travel insurance.

The most common reason we cancel is a departure that does not reach its four-traveler minimum. Where that happens you will hear from us at least 45 days before departure.

3. Changing rather than cancelling

  • Date change. Free within the first 14 days. After that treated as a cancellation and rebooking under section 1, though we will move your deposit rather than keep it whenever the supplier allows.
  • Name change. Usually possible up to 30 days before departure on group departures. Any supplier fee is passed through at cost, with the invoice shown to you.
  • Room type change. Free whenever the property has availability.
  • Adding a traveler. Free, subject to the group cap, at the price current on the day you add them.

4. What is outside this schedule

Some things we arrange are pass-through purchases made in your name, and their own supplier rules apply rather than ours:

  • Airline tickets, including any we booked for you at cost.
  • Travel insurance premiums, refundable only within the insurer's own free-look period.
  • Visa fees and government charges.
  • Pre-trip or post-trip hotel nights booked on non-refundable rates at your request. We will always tell you before booking one of these.

We will tell you which items in your quote fall into this category before you pay, and we do not mark any of them up.

5. Events outside anyone's control

If a trip becomes impossible or unsafe because of an event neither party controls, such as a natural disaster, an epidemic, war, civil unrest, a government travel ban, or airspace or border closure, we will offer you the same choice as in section 2: a transfer with the price difference waived, or a refund of everything we can recover from suppliers, plus any part of our own margin not yet spent. We will show you the supplier statements. We do not keep money in this situation as a matter of policy, but we cannot refund what a foreign supplier lawfully retains.

A change to your own circumstances, including illness, work, or a change of mind, is not covered by this section. It falls under section 1, and it is what travel insurance exists for.

6. How refunds are paid

  • Always to the original payment method. We cannot pay a refund to a different card or account.
  • Processed by us within 10 business days of the cancellation being agreed.
  • Card refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 further business days depending on your bank. ACH returns are usually faster.
  • Refunds are made in U.S. dollars for the dollar amount paid. We are not responsible for currency movement if your card is billed in another currency.
  • You will receive a written statement showing the calculation, not just a figure.

7. If you disagree with a refund calculation

Email hello@firstlighttravel.co and ask for the supplier evidence behind the figure. We will send it. If that does not resolve it, section 12 of the terms of service sets out the dispute process, including your right to bring an individual claim in small claims court.
