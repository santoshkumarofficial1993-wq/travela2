Cookie policy

What each cookie does, how long it stays, and how to change your mind. Read alongside the privacy policy.

Effective date: 1 January 2026.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store. It lets the site remember something between page loads, such as whether you have already answered the consent banner. Similar technologies including local storage, pixels and software development kits do comparable jobs, and this policy covers all of them.

2. Categories we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the website to work. These are set without consent because there is no site without them, and they carry no advertising or profiling function.

Embedded content

Our contact page embeds a map served by OpenStreetMap so you can find the office. Loading it means your IP address reaches that provider. No advertising cookie is set by the embed.

3. Your choices

  • The banner. On your first visit you can accept everything or take only what is necessary. Nothing beyond the necessary category loads until you choose.
  • Change your mind. Use the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of any page, or .
  • Your browser. Every major browser can block or delete cookies for a specific site. Blocking necessary cookies will break parts of this site, which is a limitation of the technique rather than a choice we made.
  • Personalised ads specifically. Opt out at Google Ads Settings, aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org. You will still see ads; they will simply be less relevant.
  • Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. Consent Mode

When Google tags are active on this site, we use Google Consent Mode. Declining advertising cookies sets ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization to denied, and declining analytics sets analytics_storage to denied. In that state Google's tags may still receive cookieless pings for aggregate modelling, which do not identify you and do not create an advertising profile.

5. Do we sell your data?

No. Firstlight does not sell personal information. Use of advertising cookies can amount to "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law; choosing "Necessary only" is how you opt out on this site.

6. Changes

We revise this page when we add or remove a technology. Material changes reset your stored consent, so you will be asked again rather than silently carried over.
